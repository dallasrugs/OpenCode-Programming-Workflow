# eCommerce Copywriting

Inspired by:
amanamabasiakpan/Claude-Skills-for-eCommerce

## Purpose

Create persuasive, trustworthy commerce content
that helps customers understand products and
take the appropriate next step.

Support:
- Retail and wholesale
- B2B and DTC
- Online checkout
- Inquiry-based commerce
- Quote and sample requests

Do not assume every website sells directly online.

## 1. Establish context

Before writing, identify:

- The business and brand positioning
- Target audience
- Product category
- Customer needs
- Intended channel
- Conversion objective
- Verified product information

Look for existing brand guidance in:
- .opencode/brand-guide.md
- .claude/brand-guide.md

Use ecommerce-brand-voice when appropriate.

## 2. Product descriptions

Write descriptions that explain:

- What the product is
- What distinguishes it
- Its relevant materials or construction
- Important features and specifications
- Intended use or application
- Available options when verified

Prioritize meaningful details over generic praise.

Avoid repeating the product title unnecessarily.

Never invent dimensions, materials,
manufacturing methods, certifications,
or other product attributes.

## 3. Product page copy

Help visitors answer:

1. What is this product?
2. Is it suitable for my needs?
3. What makes it different?
4. What details should I verify?
5. What should I do next?

Match calls to action to the business model.

Possible actions:
- Add to cart
- Request a quote
- Ask about availability
- Request a sample
- Contact a specialist
- Schedule a consultation

Never introduce purchase language
when checkout is unavailable.

## 4. Category and collection pages

Create useful introductions that:

- Explain the category
- Describe meaningful product differences
- Help visitors compare options
- Reflect the available assortment
- Encourage relevant exploration

Avoid repeating identical paragraphs
across different categories.

Use seo-content when search optimization
is part of the request.

## 5. Homepage and landing pages

Establish:

- Clear value proposition
- Relevant audience
- Distinct business strengths
- Supported proof points
- Logical content hierarchy
- Appropriate call to action

Be specific without exaggerating.

Avoid inflated claims of market leadership,
quality, exclusivity, or performance
without supporting evidence.

## 6. Marketing emails

Write emails with:

- A clear purpose
- Appropriate subject line
- Relevant customer context
- Natural conversational tone
- Concise supporting information
- One primary call to action

Adapt tone for:
- Product introductions
- New collections
- Customer follow-ups
- Trade inquiries
- Sample requests
- Announcements
- Promotions

Do not invent promotions, discounts,
deadlines, or available inventory.

## 7. Persuasion and trust

Use persuasion responsibly.

Emphasize real:
- Product benefits
- Differentiators
- Customer needs
- Expertise
- Services
- Guarantees, where verified
- Reviews, where verified

Do not fabricate:
- Testimonials
- Scarcity
- Urgency
- Customer results
- Awards
- Certifications
- Shipping promises

Avoid manipulative or misleading tactics.

## 8. Natural writing

Load natural-writing when appropriate.

Prefer:
- Specific vocabulary
- Natural sentence rhythm
- Clear explanations
- Audience-appropriate language
- Concise paragraphs
- Distinctive brand voice

Avoid:
- Robotic phrasing
- Repetitive templates
- Keyword stuffing
- Forced enthusiasm
- Generic marketing clichés
- Unnecessary superlatives

## 9. SEO integration

When SEO matters:

- Identify the likely search intent.
- Use relevant terminology naturally.
- Preserve factual product information.
- Suggest meaningful headings.
- Write accurate metadata.
- Recommend relevant internal links.

Use seo-content for detailed optimization.

Do not make ranking guarantees.

## 10. Multilingual content

Respect the requested language and locale.

Adapt expressions naturally rather than
translating marketing phrases word for word.

Preserve product identity and specifications.

Do not invent translated product attributes.

## 11. Review workflow

1. Understand the customer and purpose.
2. Read applicable brand guidance.
3. Verify available product facts.
4. Draft the content.
5. Check clarity and persuasion.
6. Review naturalness and brand voice.
7. Review SEO when applicable.
8. Confirm the call to action is appropriate.
9. Check that all claims are supported.

## Deliverables

Depending on the request, provide:
- Product description
- Category introduction
- Homepage or landing page copy
- Marketing email
- Product benefit summary
- Calls to action
- Copywriting improvement recommendations

## Boundaries

Do not publish content without approval.

Do not change pricing, inventory,
or product specifications.

Do not invent reviews or other evidence.

Follow the established BDFL authorization and Maintainer approval
workflow for repository changes.
