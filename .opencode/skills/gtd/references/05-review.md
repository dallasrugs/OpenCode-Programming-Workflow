# Reflect — Keeping the System Functional (Ch 8)

> Load for: the Weekly Review, "my system has gone stale," "I stopped trusting my lists," daily review, staying current.

## The Weekly Review = the critical success factor
*"The magic key to the sustainability of the process is the Weekly Review."* Without it, the system decays and you stop trusting it (and a system you don't trust, you abandon). Its purpose:
> *"Very simply, the Weekly Review is whatever you need to do to get your head empty again. It's going through the five phases of workflow management… until you can honestly say, 'I absolutely know right now everything I'm not doing but could be doing if I decided to.'"*

The goal-state is to get **clean, clear, current, and complete** — a current and complete overview of all your open loops, risen up to at least the "10,000-foot" (current-projects) level.

## The Weekly Review drill (the checklist)
**Get clear**
1. **Collect loose papers & materials** — gather stray notes, cards, receipts into "in."
2. **Process your notes** — meeting notes, scribbles → actions, projects, waiting-fors, calendar, someday/maybes; file reference. *Be ruthless.*
3. **Empty your head** — dump any new projects/actions/waiting-fors/someday-maybes not yet captured.

**Get current**
4. **Review previous calendar** — mine past dates for leftover actions, follow-ups, reference; capture them. Archive the week "with nothing left uncaptured."
5. **Review upcoming calendar** — near and long term; capture prep/arrangements now.
6. **Review Waiting For** — note follow-ups needed; check off what arrived.
7. **Review Projects (and larger outcomes) one by one** — confirm **each project has at least one current next action** in your system; check status.
8. **Review Next Actions lists** — mark off done; add any new next steps.
9. **Review any relevant checklists** — *"Is there anything you haven't done that you need to do?"*

**Get creative**
10. **Review Someday/Maybe** — promote anything now active to Projects; delete what's dead.
11. **Review Pending & Support files** — browse work-in-progress to trigger new actions.
12. **Be creative and courageous** — *"Any new, wonderful, harebrained, creative, thought-provoking, risk-taking ideas you can add to your system?"*

## When & how long
- **Weekly.** Allen recommends **~2 hours, Friday afternoon**, because: the week is fresh enough for a clean postmortem; there's still time to reach people before the weekend; and you *"clear your psychic decks"* to enjoy the weekend *"with nothing on your mind."*
- Non-standard schedule? Build your own **weekly regrouping ritual** (a recurring quiet block — a flight, Sunday night).
- *"Your best thoughts about work won't happen while you're at work."*

## Daily review
- **Look at your calendar first** — the "hard landscape" (plus today's tickler folder): the immovable time/space commitments.
- **Then your Next Actions lists** — for your current context, choose the best next action.
- *"A few seconds a day is usually all you need for review, as long as you're looking at the right things at the right time."*

## How to apply
- If the user's system feels stale/untrusted, the prescription is almost always: **do a Weekly Review.** Offer to walk them through the 12 steps against their actual lists, or run it interactively.
- The non-negotiable check in any review: **does every project have a defined next action?** Projects without next actions are where trust (and momentum) leak away.
- If a full weekly habit is too much at first, anchor the two highest-value steps: **empty your head** + **every project has a next action.**
